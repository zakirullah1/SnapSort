1. The short version

  • SnapSort reads your photos and screenshots only on your phone to organize them, search their text and find duplicates.
  • We do not upload, copy or sell your photos, screenshots or the text found in them.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number to use the App.
  • We use third-party services for ads, crash reports, analytics and purchases. These collect limited technical data, described in Section 4.
  • You decide what gets deleted. SnapSort never deletes a photo without your confirmation.

2. Information the App accesses on your device

2.1 Photos and screenshots

With your permission, the App reads images in your device gallery (or only the screenshots, depending on the scan setting you choose, or a folder you pick). For each image the App creates a local record containing:

  • file reference, date, size and dimensions
  • text recognized in the image (OCR)
  • a category (for example Receipts, Chats, Tickets, Memes, Notes, Photos) and confidence score
  • a visual fingerprint (perceptual hash) used to find similar images
  • items detected in the text, such as dates, phone numbers, web links, email addresses and street addresses
  • your own marks, such as favorites and "keep" decisions

All of this is processed on your device, using on-device tools (including Google ML Kit text recognition, image labeling and entity extraction, which run locally). This information is stored in a private database inside the App and is not sent to our servers or anyone else.

2.3 Deleting photos

When you confirm a cleanup, the App asks Android to delete the selected files. Android may show its own confirmation. Deleted items may be moved to your device's trash according to your device settings. We cannot restore files for you.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • scan, organize, search and clean up your images
  • show suggested actions (calendar, contact, link, email, map)
  • show your cleanup totals (for example, space freed)
  • provide ads in the free version and remove them for premium users
  • process and verify purchases and restore premium access
  • keep the App stable, fix crashes and improve features
  • comply with the law and protect against fraud or abuse

We do not use your photos or recognized text for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

5. Advertising and consent

The free version shows ads. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the App settings (if available) or in your Android ads settings. If you do not consent to personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Premium subscribers and purchasers do not see ads.

6. Purchases

Premium plans are sold through Google Play. Payments, renewals, refunds and cancellations are managed by Google Play under Google's terms. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in the Google Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

7. Data storage, retention and deletion

  • On your device: the index of your images (Section 2.1) stays in the App's private storage until you use Settings, Clear index, clear the App's data in Android settings, or uninstall the App. Clearing the index does not delete your original photos.
  • Analytics and crash data: retained by Google according to the retention settings of Firebase, typically for a limited period.
  • Advertising data: retained by the advertising provider under its own policies.
  • We do not hold a copy of your images or a user profile on our servers, so there is no account or cloud data for us to delete. To ask questions about data held by us, contact kz5008489@gmail.com.

8. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited technical data only with the service providers listed in Section 4, and we may disclose information if required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect rights, safety and security.

If you tap "Share my result", the App creates a card that shows your cleanup totals and shares it using the app you choose. It does not include your photos.

9. Children

SnapSort is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact kz5008489@gmail.com and we will help to remove it.

Image data is processed and stored on your device and is protected by your device's security (such as screen lock and Android app sandboxing). Data sent to our service providers is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
